# NyayaSutra

## Legal Case and Backlog Management System

NyayaSutra is a web-based portal designed to help lawyers manage their legal workload more efficiently.

The system does not replace the lawyer's legal knowledge or decision-making. Instead, it organizes case information, hearings, clients, tasks and important dates so that lawyers can focus on their legal work.

## Main Objectives

- Manage legal cases
- Track pending and active cases
- Track upcoming hearings
- Manage client information
- Manage daily legal tasks
- Provide a backlog overview
- Help lawyers stay aware of upcoming dates
- Improve organization of legal work
